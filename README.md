# Reeves Restaurant Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

Reeves is a restaurant business operating across multiple outlets in Bengaluru, serving customers through Walk-in, Zomato, Swiggy, and Direct Delivery channels.

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales and operational data over three years (2023–2025), providing insights into sales performance, outlet operations, product trends, customer behaviour, and channel profitability.

## Business Problem

Management needs a centralized view of business performance to identify top-performing outlets and products, compare sales channels, understand customer behaviour, and evaluate the impact of discounts and platform commissions on profitability.

## Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and order performance.
- Identify top-performing outlets and products.
- Compare revenue and profitability across sales channels.
- Understand customer activity, demographics, and satisfaction.
- Analyze order patterns and the impact of discounts and platform commissions.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive dashboards.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** — dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX** — measures and business calculations
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation
- **Data Modeling** — relationships between customers, orders, products, outlets, and dates
- **Microsoft Excel** — dataset preparation and storage

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Overview** — key performance indicators and overall sales trends
2. **Sales Overview** — sales trends, channel performance, top products, top outlets, and daily sales patterns
3. **Outlet Analysis** — outlet rankings, monthly sales trends, and performance comparisons
4. **Product Analysis** — top-selling products, category performance, and quantities sold
5. **Customer Analysis** — customer activity, demographics, ratings, and spending patterns
6. **Channel Analysis** — revenue, average order value, commissions, discounts, and profit by channel
7. **Orders Analysis** — order statuses, payment methods, time slots, and recent orders

## Key Business Questions

- Which outlets and products generate the highest sales?
- How do sales channels compare in revenue and profitability?
- What is the impact of discounts and platform commissions on profitability?
- How do customer activity and satisfaction vary across the business?
- How do sales and order patterns change over time?
- Which areas of the business may offer opportunities for improvement?

## Dataset

The dataset covers the period 2023–2025 and includes information about:

- Orders and order details
- Customers and customer demographics
- Products, categories, selling prices, and food costs
- Outlets and their locations
- Sales channels and payment methods
- Order statuses, discounts, delivery charges, and platform commissions
- Customer ratings and order times

The dataset was prepared for portfolio and analytical practice. Business findings should be interpreted within the scope and assumptions of the dataset.
